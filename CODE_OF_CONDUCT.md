# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, by reference. The full text is published by the
Contributor Covenant project at:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

It applies to every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments) and to anyone who
represents the project in public. The text there, not this summary, is the binding statement. The Contributor
Covenant is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) by the Organization for Ethical Source.

## In short

- Be considerate, patient and constructive. Criticise code, models and arguments, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and people with different backgrounds, experience and first languages.
- Accept and give feedback gracefully; take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Do not harass, demean, threaten or discriminate. Do not make sexualised remarks, post other people's
  private information, or keep contacting someone who has asked you to stop.
- Keep the focus on the work. Scientific disagreement is normal and healthy; disrespect is not.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem:

- Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the offending comment, issue or pull request, which reaches GitHub
  and the repository's maintainers; or
- open an issue in the repository's issue tracker
  (<https://github.com/pandakingpunc/fusion-reactor-simulator/issues>) that says only that you would like to
  raise a conduct concern and asks a maintainer for a private way to talk. Do not put the details, names or
  evidence in the public issue.

Reports are handled with discretion, and the reporter's privacy is respected. Maintainers follow the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) when they decide
on a consequence.

A security vulnerability is not a conduct matter; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for that.
